# Broker Outreach Playbook

Goal: 3 paid pilots (AED 499 first month, then AED 1,499/mo) within 2 weeks of the demo line going live.

## Target criteria

- Dubai brokerages or solo agents actively listing on Bayut / Property Finder
- 3–20 agents (solo brokers churn more; big agencies have sales teams)
- Signs of missed leads: listings with "Agent not responding" reviews, slow WhatsApp replies, ads running after-hours

Build the list manually: Bayut agent pages, Instagram real-estate accounts, LinkedIn titles containing "Real Estate Consultant" + Dubai. Log name, agency, phone/WhatsApp, portal activity in a Google Sheet.

## Positioning line

> We answer every lead in under 60 seconds, qualify it, and book the viewing - in English and Arabic. You only talk to qualified, booked clients.

## WhatsApp / DM opener

Salam [Name], saw your [area] listings on Bayut - strong inventory.

Quick one: when a lead comes in while you're mid-viewing, who answers?

We built an AI that picks up every call instantly, qualifies the buyer (budget, area, timeline), and books the viewing into your calendar. It just booked viewings on our live demo line.

Want the number? Call it right now and try to catch it slipping. If it impresses you, I'll set up your own line this week - first month AED 499 instead of 1,499.

## Cold-call opener (20 seconds)

"Hi [Name], I'll be quick - I know leads go cold fast in your business. When a Bayut inquiry comes at 9pm and nobody answers, that client calls the next broker. We put an AI on your line that answers in under a minute, qualifies them, books the viewing, and texts you the summary. Call our demo line right now: [number]. Try it in Arabic too. Two minutes - worth it?"

Then stop talking. Let them call.

## The demo IS the pitch

Never explain the product twice. Every conversation ends with: "Call this number now." The product closes for you. After they hang up, ask:

1. "Would your clients know it's AI?"
2. "What would 10 extra booked viewings a month be worth to you?"

## Objection handling

**"I already have a CRM."**
"Perfect - keep it. We're the layer before the CRM: we make sure the lead actually gets answered and arrives in your pipeline qualified and booked."

**"AI sounds robotic / my clients will hate it."**
"You just called it - could you tell? Also: they only meet it when your team can't pick up. The alternative isn't you answering, it's voicemail."

**"Does it speak Arabic properly?"**
"Gulf dialects, and it code-switches mid-sentence like Dubai callers do. Test it right now in Arabic - I'll wait."

**"Is client data safe? RERA concerns?"**
"We only capture what a receptionist would: name, number, requirement, budget. Stored encrypted, never sold, deleted on request. Full transcript stays yours."

**"Too expensive."**
"One closed deal pays for 30 months. And if it doesn't perform, you cancel - no contract."
